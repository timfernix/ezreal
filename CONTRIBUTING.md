# Contributing to the Ezreal Media Archive

Thanks for helping make this archive better! This guide explains what you can submit, how to share it, and what I can accept.

> **TL;DR**
>
> - Only **official Riot Games** media (no fanart/3rd-party, unless you are the rights holder asking for removal).
> - You can **open a GitHub Issue** or **DM me on Twitter** with your contribution.
> - Include: All information you think is useful and a **source link** (or the file itself).

---

## What you can contribute

- **Missing media**: splasharts, icons, promo/key art, concept sheets, loadingscreens, model shots, chromas, emotes, GIF files, videos, YouTube links or anything "Other" (items that don’t fit elsewhere).
- **Higher-resolution versions** of existing items.
- **Platform variants**: Wild Rift (**WR**), Teamfight Tactics (**TFT**), Legends of Runeterra (**LoR**).
- **Corrections**: wrong title, wrong skin/category, missing tags, or broken links.
- **Takedown requests**: if you are the rights holder/artist and want something removed.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Official media only.** I cannot include fanmade or third-party works due to copyright.  
> If you are an artist/rights holder and want a removal, please see the **Removal requests** section below.

---

## How to contribute

### Option A: Open a GitHub Issue (recommended, GitHub account needed)

1. Go to **[New Issue → Choose a template](https://github.com/timfernix/ezreal/issues/new/choose)**.
2. Pick **“Media request / correction”** (or open a blank issue if needed).
3. Include the following (if possible):

#### For new media

- **Skin**: e.g. *Arcade Ezreal*  
- **Type**: Splash / Icon / Promo / Concept / Loading / Model / Chroma / Ingame / Emote / GIF / Video / YouTube / Merch  
- **Tags**: `WR`, `TFT`, `LoR`
- **Source**: official link(s) (Universe, press kit, YouTube, link, etc.)  
- **(Optional) Title**: if you prefer a specific title (otherwise it’s inferred from the filename)
- **(Optional) Notes**: any context (e.g., “this is the updated splash released in 2021”)

#### For corrections

- **What to change**: title, type, tags, or link  
- **Current entry**: link to the item in the site/repo  
- **What it should be**: and why (include source if possible)

#### For removals (rights holders)

- **Which item(s)**: link to the item(s) in the site/repo  
- **Proof of authorship/rights**: e.g., link to your original post, portfolio, or other verification  
- **Action requested**: removal or other change

You can also say things like:

> - “I have a higher-resolution image of *Classic Ezreal* splash.”  
> - “I’m the artist of this media—please remove it.”  
> - “Please rename this file/title to …”  
> If your issue is something else entirely, feel free to **remove the template text** and write it in your own words.

---

### Option B: DM me on Twitter

If you prefer a private message: **[@timfernix](https://x.com/timfernix)**  
Please include the same details as the issue template (skin, type, tags, source, etc.).  
I’ll acknowledge your message and handle the addition/correction/takedown.

---

## Removal requests (rights holders)

If you are the creator or rights holder of media shown here and want it removed:

1. Open an **Issue** or **DM me** (see above).
2. Include links to the items to remove and **proof of authorship/rights** (e.g., official post, portfolio, etc.).
3. I will review and remove promptly.
